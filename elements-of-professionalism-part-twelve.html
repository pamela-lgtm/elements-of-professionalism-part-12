The Elements of Professionalism, Part Twelve

Consistency

Friday, November 20, 2026

Hi Team,

Most of what I have written to you so far has been about doing things well. This week is about doing them well every time.

Anyone can do great work on a good day, with an easy owner and a quiet inbox. What owners are really paying us for is getting the same quality on the hard days too. The busy week. The difficult owner. The Friday afternoon when you’re tired and want to get out the door.

Owners notice the gaps

An owner will forgive a lot if they know what to expect from us. What wears down their trust is not one bad experience. It is never being sure which version of us they are going to get.

If we return one call in an hour and the next one in two days, the owner does not remember the fast one. They remember wondering if we got the message. If one month’s statement is clean and the next one has questions nobody answered, they start checking everything we send. Once an owner starts checking our work, the relationship has changed.

Every owner gets the same Verandah

This one is important to me. It is easy to give extra attention to the owner who is pleasant and appreciative, and to do the minimum for the one who is demanding. But every owner signed the same agreement and is paying for the same standard.

The same goes for properties. A one-bedroom condo gets the same care as a five-bedroom home in Laureate Park. The size of the rent does not change the size of our standard.

What consistency looks like day to day

  • Follow the process every time, even when you know it well enough to skip a step. Most mistakes happen on the steps we skip because we are sure we don’t need them.
  • Use the checklists and templates we have. They exist so the work comes out the same no matter who does it or how busy the day is.
  • Keep the same tone with everyone. Calm and professional with the easy owner and the upset one.
  • Hold the same standard at the end of the week as you do at the start. A Friday email gets the same care as a Monday email.
  • If you find a better way to do something, share it. Don’t quietly do it your own way. A better process only helps if the whole team uses it.

Why it matters for us as a company

We are building Verandah so that the quality an owner gets does not depend on who happens to answer the phone that day. That is what the processes, checklists and ClickUp work have all been about. They only work if we use them every single time, not just when we remember.

Consistency is also what lets us grow without losing what makes us different. A small company can get by on a few people working hard and remembering everything. A company that keeps its standard as it grows does it by doing the same things the same way, every time.

Ask yourself this week
  1. Would every owner get the same answer from me that I just gave this one?
  2. Did I skip any steps today because I was busy or sure I didn’t need them?
  3. If someone else had done this task, would it have come out the same way?

None of this is about being perfect. It is about being reliable. Owners stay with us for years when they know exactly what they are going to get, and so do the people they refer to us.

Thank you for the work you put in every day.
